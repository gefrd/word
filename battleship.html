How to play

  • Place your ships: tap a ship below the board, then tap a square. Drag a ship to move it, tap it to turn it. 🎲 places them all at random.
  • Ships can't touch each other, not even at a corner.
  • Take turns firing at the enemy grid. Tap a square to aim, tap it again (or 🎯 FIRE) to shoot.
  • ✕ is a hit, a dot is a miss. When a ship sinks, the water around it is marked too.
  • Sink the whole enemy fleet to win.
