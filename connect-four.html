How to play

  • Tap a column to drop your disc. It falls to the lowest free spot.
  • Get 4 of your discs in a row — across, up and down, or diagonally — to win.
  • If the board fills up and nobody has 4 in a row, it is a draw.
  • Tip: block your opponent when they have 3 in a row with a free spot next to it.
