How to play

  • Tap one of your pieces, then tap a glowing square to move it.
  • Men move one square diagonally forward.
  • Capturing is compulsory. Jump over an enemy piece next to you onto the free square behind it — forwards or backwards. Keep jumping while you can.
  • Official rule: if you can capture in several ways, you must take the most pieces.
  • A man that ends its move on the far row becomes a king 👑.
  • Kings fly: they move and capture any distance along a diagonal and may land on any free square behind the piece they take.
  • Captured pieces are removed after the move, and a piece cannot be jumped twice.
  • You win when your opponent cannot move — no pieces left, or all blocked.
  • Draw: the same position three times; 25 moves each with only kings and no captures; or a lone king against 3 pieces (16 moves each) or against 1–2 pieces (5 moves each).

Long capture: tap the last square, or tap each landing square in turn. Tap the board during a move to skip the animation.
