How to play

  • Get rid of all your cards. The last player holding cards is the Durak (the fool).
  • The suit of the card under the deck is trump. The player with the lowest trump attacks first.
  • Attack: tap cards of one rank, then ⚔️ Attack. The player on your left defends.
  • Defend: tap your card, then tap the card on the table it beats: a higher card of the same suit, or any trump (trumps are marked with a gold dot).
  • When all cards are beaten, attackers may throw in more cards of ranks already on the table (at most 6, and no more than the defender holds). Tap ✅ Done when you have nothing to add.
  • If you can't or don't want to beat, tap 📥 Take: you pick up all the cards and miss your attack.
  • Transfer game: before beating anything, the defender may add a card of the same rank to pass the attack on to the next player.
  • After each round everyone draws back up to 6 cards, the attacker first.
