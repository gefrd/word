How to play

  • Each player owns the 2 rows of pits on their side (16 pits).
  • Tap one of your glowing pits. Its seeds are sown one by one along your pits, anticlockwise.
  • If the last seed falls into a non-empty pit, you pick those seeds up and keep sowing.
  • If the last seed falls into a non-empty pit in your inner row (next to the middle line) and the opponent's pits in front of it have seeds, you capture them and sow them on your side.
  • When the last seed falls into an empty pit, your turn ends.
  • A player who cannot make a move loses.
