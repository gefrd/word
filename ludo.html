How to play

  • Tap the dice to roll. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard.
  • Tap a bouncing token to move it. Tokens go clockwise around the board, then up your coloured path to the middle.
  • Land on another colour to send it back to its yard. Squares with a ★ and the start squares are safe.
  • A 6 gives you another roll. You need the exact number to reach home.
  • The first player to bring their tokens home wins.
