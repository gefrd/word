How to play

  • Play a card with the same colour, number or symbol as the top card. Bright cards in your hand can be played.
  • No card fits? Tap the deck (or Draw). If the new card fits you may play it, or keep it.
  • ⊘ Skip: the next player misses a turn. ⇄ Reverse: change direction (with 2 players it works like Skip). +2: the next player takes 2 cards and misses a turn.
  • Wild: choose the next colour. Wild +4: choose the colour, and the next player takes 4 cards.
  • When you have 2 cards, tap UNO! before you play one. Forget, and you take 2 cards.
  • The first player with no cards left wins.
